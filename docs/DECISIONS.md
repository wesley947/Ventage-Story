**Countdown clarification (2026-11-08):** The 30-second ingestion timer is a **resettable inactivity countdown, not an additive extension**. Each successful bite while a session is open sets `deadline = currentRealTime + 30 seconds` (equivalently, `remaining = 30s`), NOT `deadline += 30 seconds`. Thus 12 carrots cannot create a 6-minute *remaining* timer; the maximum remaining countdown at any instant is 30s. Continuous eating can still keep a session active for many minutes. When 30s of silence elapse, close and timestamp the entire meal at that closure. Keep per-food identities and quantities. This is a user-explicit correction; avoid conflating long total session duration with additive timer behavior.

| D45 | **Separate player and animal intake policies while sharing one digestion/reservoir engine.** Players eat frequently and use their mixed-food 30-second inactivity batching; animals often go unfed for long periods in vanilla, so directly observed feedings use per-event contributions, **plus subtle species-specific background intestinal gas** even without observed food. | **User requirement (2026-11-08), architecture proposed and not implemented.** Do not add animal starvation, force animal feeding timers, invent food identities from satiety, or impose player-style eating sessions on animal AI. Respect realistic differences among species, herd noise limits and compatibility with husbandry mods. |

| D46 | **Hard conservation invariant for players and terrestrial animals:** Stored intestinal gas continuously accumulates from delayed food fermentation and/or background gas, without pausing or disappearing due to cooldown, source expiry, elapsed time, save/reload or capacity. **Only actual flatulence releases remove part or all of the gas reservoir.** | **Explicit user requirement (2026-11-08).** Release cooldowns apply to release frequency, never production. High-pressure thresholds must not silently clamp/delete excess; gas-lowering foods may only change *future generation* in v1, not absorb already-stored gas. Persist precisely across state transitions and test conservation. |

| D47 | Define **100% as normal "Full" pressure, not a storage cap**. Avoid practical accumulation far above 125% while conserving gas strictly. | **Explicit user target (2026-11-08), detailed curve/cadence not finalized.** Proposed failsafe: at >=125% prioritize/guarantee a genuine large release, possibly bypassing ordinary cooldown. No silent clamp, disappearance, per-frame chance artifacts, or unlogged removal. |

| D48 | Include natural random **flatulence opportunities during standing/ordinary activity** plus **extra distinct event-driven rolls** for meaningful motion, particularly jumping, crouching and standing transitions, when there is real accumulated reservoir gas (proposed threshold ~25% of normal full). No failed roll consumes gas. | **User requirement (2026-11-08), details not yet tuned.** Proposed implementation: coarse time-based opportunities per entity (not per frame), movement/activity weighting, debounced one-off action events, existing deliberate squat relief respected, cooldown limits actual release only; use independent probability and release-amount selection.
